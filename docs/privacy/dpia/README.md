# Data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)

A DPIA (GDPR Art 35) is required before processing that is likely to result in a high risk.
The DPC's list and the EDPB criteria point to these features of Offerlight:

| DPIA | Features | Why | Must be signed off before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `0001-matching-scores-and-insights.md` | Matching, honest-rejection scores, insights, benchmarks | Evaluation/scoring (profiling) at scale, in an employment context | Stage 6 launch |
| `0002-interview-practice-recordings.md` | Voice and video practice | Voice data, new technology, possible inference about the person | Stage 10 launch |
| `0003-fairness-audit-demographics.md` | Fairness audit | Special category data (Art 9) | Stage 11 |

Each file is a structured draft. Owners complete it with the solicitor/DPO; if residual
risk stays high after mitigation, consult the DPC before launch (Art 36).

**Template sections:** 1 Description of processing · 2 Necessity and proportionality ·
3 Risks to people (likelihood × severity) · 4 Measures · 5 Residual risk · 6 Sign-off.
