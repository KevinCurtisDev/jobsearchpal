# Privacy notice — outline for the solicitor

Plain language, layered: a one-screen summary, then sections. Reading age around 12.
Every fact below comes from `records-of-processing.md`; when the inventory changes, the
notice is reviewed (PR checklist).

## Summary (first screen)
- Who we are and how to contact us (privacy@, DPO if appointed, UK representative).
- We never sell your data, never show it to employers, and never use it for ads.
- What we use your data for, in five bullets.
- Your choices: switches for benchmarks, experiments, voice practice, product news.
- Download or delete everything in Settings → Privacy, any time.

## Sections
1. Who we are (controller, Irish establishment, DPC as lead authority; ICO for UK)
2. What we collect and why — one row per category: data, purpose, lawful basis, how long
3. AI features — what is sent (never your contact details), to whom, that suggestions are
   reviewed by you, that nothing is invented (EU AI Act transparency)
4. Scores and matching — advisory only, how they work, no automated decisions about you
5. Interview practice — video never leaves your device; audio deleted after transcription
6. Who we share with — processor list, locations, transfer safeguards
7. How long we keep it — retention table, backups 30 days
8. Your rights — access, export, correct, delete, restrict, object, withdraw consent,
   complain to the DPC (dataprotection.ie) or ICO (ico.org.uk)
9. Cookies — strictly necessary only (session, CSRF); no banner needed; cookieless analytics
10. Age — you must be 16 or over
11. **For employers and recruiters** — replies to candidates and public job ads (Art 14 notice)
12. Changes to this notice — versioned; material changes notified by email in advance
